What's Due

Everything you still need to turn in, across all your Canvas courses, in one list.

Where do I find it?
  1. Open Canvas Calendar.
  2. At the bottom right, click Calendar Feed.
  3. Copy the link it shows and paste it above.

The link lets this app see your Canvas calendar: assignment names and due dates. It stays saved, encrypted, so you stay logged in, until you click Log out. Keep it private, like a password.
